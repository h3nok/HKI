# Code of Conduct

HKI contributors are expected to keep technical discussion direct, respectful,
and focused on the work.

## Standards

- Be precise when describing bugs, risks, and tradeoffs.
- Critique code, behavior, and architecture rather than people.
- Assume public project spaces are shared professional spaces.
- Avoid harassment, threats, doxxing, sexualized language, discriminatory
  language, or repeated disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, block accounts, or decline
contributions that violate these standards. Serious security reports should
follow `SECURITY.md`, not public discussion threads.
