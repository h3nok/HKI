## Prescription Transfers
To transfer a prescription from another pharmacy, provide: pharmacy name and phone number, prescription number, and medication name. Our pharmacist will handle the rest. Controlled substances Schedule III-V can be transferred one time. Schedule II cannot be transferred.

## Refill Options
1. **Online**: hkipharmacy.com — log in with membership number
2. **Mobile App**: HKI app → Pharmacy → Scan the barcode on your label
3. **Phone**: Call pharmacy directly or use the automated refill line
4. **In-Person**: Drop off your empty bottle at the pharmacy counter

## Pricing
HKI Pharmacy is open to the public — no membership is required for pharmacy services. The HKI Member Prescription Program offers additional savings on select generic medications. Compare pricing at hki.com/pharmacy-pricing.
