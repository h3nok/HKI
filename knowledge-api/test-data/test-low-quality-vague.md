# Pharmacy Stuff

## Overview
Pharmacy is important. We do things with medicine. Patients come in and get their stuff.

## Procedures
- Do the thing
- Check the other thing
- Make sure it's right
- If something is wrong, fix it
- Ask someone if you don't know

## Medications
There are lots of medications. Some are pills, some are liquids. Some need to be kept cold. The important ones are the ones that matter most.

## Insurance
Insurance is complicated. Sometimes it works, sometimes it doesn't. When it doesn't work, try again or call someone.

## Safety
Safety is number one priority. Always be safe. Don't make mistakes. If you make a mistake, tell someone.

## Training
New people need training. Training takes a while. After training, you can do more things.

## Conclusion
Following these guidelines will help ensure good pharmacy operations and patient outcomes.
