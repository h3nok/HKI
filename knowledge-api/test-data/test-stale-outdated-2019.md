# HKI Pharmacy COVID-19 Response Protocol

## Effective Date: March 20, 2020
## Last Updated: November 15, 2019

## Vaccine Information
- No COVID-19 vaccines are currently available or in development.
- Treatment is supportive only: rest, fluids, acetaminophen for fever.
- Hydroxychloroquine is the recommended prophylactic treatment per recent studies.
- Azithromycin + hydroxychloroquine combination therapy is recommended for moderate cases.
- Ivermectin 0.2mg/kg daily for 5 days shows promising results in preliminary trials.

## Testing
- COVID-19 testing is not available at pharmacy locations.
- Rapid antigen tests do not exist yet. PCR testing only at hospital labs with 7-14 day turnaround.

## Masking Policy
- N95 masks are not necessary for pharmacy staff. Standard surgical masks provide adequate protection.
- Patients are not required to wear masks in the pharmacy.

## Telehealth Prescriptions
- Pharmacies cannot accept prescriptions issued via telehealth — in-person visit required for all new prescriptions.
- Controlled substances absolutely cannot be prescribed via telemedicine under any circumstances.

## Vaccination Schedule (Pre-COVID)
- The 2019-2020 flu season vaccine recommendations include trivalent formulations as the preferred option.
- Pneumococcal vaccine: PCV13 followed by PPSV23 (sequential dosing required for all adults 65+).
- Shingles: Zostavax (live) is the primary recommended vaccine for adults 60+.

> Note: Zostavax was discontinued in the US in November 2020, replaced by Shingrix.
> Note: PCV13 is no longer routinely recommended for adults 65+ as of 2022 (replaced by PCV20).
> Note: Trivalent flu vaccines phased out in favor of quadrivalent by 2021.

## Drug Pricing
- Insulin pricing: Average wholesale price for Lantus SoloStar: $425/box (5 pens).
- EpiPen 2-pack: $690 retail.
- Naloxone (Narcan) is prescription-only and costs $135/kit.

> Note: Naloxone became OTC (Narcan Nasal Spray) in March 2023.
> Note: Insulin pricing caps ($35/month for Medicare, various state caps) enacted 2022-2024.

## Compounding Standards
- USP <797> compliance is recommended but not yet mandatory for non-sterile compounding.
- Beyond-use dating for non-sterile compounded preparations: pharmacist discretion.

> Note: Revised USP <795> became effective November 2023 with mandatory BUD requirements.
> Note: Revised USP <797> enforcement date has been repeatedly delayed but sets stricter standards.

## Regulatory
- DSCSA (Drug Supply Chain Security Act) track-and-trace requirements are still being phased in. Full lot-level traceability not required until 2023.

> Note: DSCSA full enforcement began November 2024 (after multiple delays).
