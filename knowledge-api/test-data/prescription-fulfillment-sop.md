# Prescription Fulfillment Standard Operating Procedure

## Purpose
This document outlines the standard operating procedure for fulfilling prescriptions at HKI Pharmacy locations. All pharmacy staff must follow these steps to ensure accuracy, compliance, and patient safety.

## Scope
Applies to all HKI Pharmacy technicians, pharmacists, and pharmacy interns across all warehouse locations.

## Procedure

### 1. Prescription Intake
- Accept prescriptions via the following channels: in-person drop-off, phone-in, fax, e-prescribe (electronic), or transfer from another pharmacy.
- Verify patient identity using full name, date of birth, and address.
- Confirm prescriber information: name, DEA number (for controlled substances), NPI, phone number.
- Check prescription validity: date written (must be within 1 year for non-controlled, 6 months for Schedule II), refills authorized, and quantity.
- Log the prescription into the pharmacy management system (PMS) immediately upon receipt.

### 2. Insurance Verification & Adjudication
- Run the prescription through the patient's primary insurance.
- If rejected, check for secondary insurance or discount programs (e.g., HKI Member Prescription Program).
- Common rejection codes and resolutions:
  - **75 (Prior Authorization Required)**: Contact prescriber to initiate PA with insurer.
  - **76 (Plan Limitations Exceeded)**: Check if vacation supply override is applicable.
  - **79 (Refill Too Soon)**: Verify last fill date; patient may use a different pharmacy.
  - **70 (Product Not Covered)**: Suggest therapeutic alternative to prescriber.
- Document all insurance communications in the patient profile.

### 3. Data Entry & Verification
- Enter drug name, strength, dosage form, quantity, directions (SIG), and DAW code.
- Cross-reference the entered data against the original prescription image.
- Flag any discrepancies, incomplete directions, or potential errors for pharmacist review.
- System will automatically check for:
  - Drug-drug interactions
  - Drug-allergy interactions
  - Duplicate therapy
  - Age/weight appropriateness

### 4. Filling
- Pull the correct medication from inventory using NDC barcode verification.
- Count or measure the prescribed quantity.
- For compounded prescriptions, follow the compounding log and have a pharmacist verify each step.
- Apply the prescription label with: patient name, drug name/strength, quantity, directions, prescriber name, pharmacy info, refill count, and any auxiliary labels.

### 5. Pharmacist Verification
- The pharmacist must verify every prescription before dispensing.
- Verification includes: correct drug, correct strength, correct quantity, correct patient, correct directions, and appropriate clinical review.
- Initial the verification log (electronic or physical).

### 6. Patient Counseling & Dispensing
- Offer counseling to every patient (required by OBRA '90).
- Key counseling points: purpose of medication, how to take it, common side effects, storage requirements, and what to do if a dose is missed.
- For new medications, counseling is mandatory in most states.
- Collect patient signature for HIPAA acknowledgment and counseling offer.
- Dispense medication and provide printed medication guide if applicable.

## Controlled Substance Additional Requirements
- Schedule II prescriptions: no refills allowed, must be a new prescription each time.
- Schedule III-V prescriptions: up to 5 refills within 6 months of the date written.
- CURES/PDMP check required before dispensing any Schedule II-IV substance.
- Maintain perpetual inventory for Schedule II medications.
- Report any discrepancies to the Pharmacy Manager immediately.

## Error Reporting
- Any dispensing error, near-miss, or deviation from this SOP must be reported via the Pharmacy Incident Report system within 24 hours.
- Root cause analysis will be conducted for all errors reaching the patient.
- No punitive action for good-faith error reporting.
