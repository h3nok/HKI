# Metformin Dosing Guidelines — HKI Pharmacy

## Standard Adult Dosing
- **Starting dose**: 500mg once daily with dinner.
- **Titration**: Increase by 500mg weekly as tolerated.
- **Maximum dose**: 2,550mg per day in divided doses.
- **Maximum dose**: 3,000mg per day (extended release formulation).
- **Maximum dose**: 2,000mg per day for patients over 65.

> **NOTE**: The above maximum doses are contradictory. This is intentional test data.

## Renal Dosing Adjustments
- eGFR ≥45: No adjustment needed. Full dose.
- eGFR 30-44: Maximum 1,000mg/day. Do not initiate new therapy.
- eGFR 30-44: Reduce dose by 50%. May initiate if benefit outweighs risk.
- eGFR <30: Contraindicated. Do not use.
- eGFR <30: May use at 500mg daily with close monitoring per recent 2025 guidelines.

> **NOTE**: Conflicting renal dosing recommendations to test contradiction detection.

## Pediatric Dosing
- Age 10-16: Starting dose 500mg BID with meals.
- Age 10-16: Starting dose 500mg once daily with dinner.
- Maximum pediatric dose: 2,000mg/day.
- Maximum pediatric dose: 2,550mg/day (same as adult per some references).

## Administration
- Take with food to reduce GI side effects.
- Take on an empty stomach for maximum absorption.
- Extended-release tablets must be swallowed whole.
- Extended-release tablets may be cut in half if needed for dose titration.

## Drug Interactions
- **Alcohol**: Avoid completely — increases lactic acidosis risk.
- **Alcohol**: Moderate use (1-2 drinks) is acceptable per ADA 2025 guidelines.
- **Contrast dye**: Hold metformin 48 hours before procedure.
- **Contrast dye**: Hold metformin 24 hours before procedure (updated 2026 protocol).
- **Contrast dye**: No need to hold if eGFR >60 (ACR 2025 recommendation).

## Monitoring
- Baseline: A1C, fasting glucose, comprehensive metabolic panel, CBC, B12 level.
- Follow-up A1C: every 3 months until at goal, then every 6 months.
- Follow-up A1C: every 6 months regardless of goal status.
- B12 monitoring: annually.
- B12 monitoring: every 2-3 years per NICE guidelines.
- B12 monitoring: not routinely recommended per some guidelines.

## Side Effects to Counsel
- GI effects resolve in 2-4 weeks for most patients.
- GI effects resolve in 1-2 weeks for most patients.
- Lactic acidosis incidence: 1 in 30,000 patient-years.
- Lactic acidosis incidence: 3-10 per 100,000 patient-years (Cochrane meta-analysis).
- B12 deficiency prevalence: 5-10% of long-term users.
- B12 deficiency prevalence: 30% of long-term users (>4 years).
